FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I still need my phone during a match?
No. Scores, live insights and moment alerts appear on your Fire TV. Your phone is only needed for the initial setup.
Which sports are supported?
All four sports are in the live demo: football, cricket, basketball and tennis. More sports are on the roadmap.
Can everyone in the family have their own profile?
Yes. Each profile keeps its own teams, highlights and alert preferences, so everyone sees what matters to them.
Can I choose which moments interrupt me?
Yes. In the Alerts tab of the demo you can turn each moment type on or off, or switch on quiet mode to pause automatic pop-ups.
When will pricing and launch details be announced?
Early access members will hear about launch timing and pricing first.
